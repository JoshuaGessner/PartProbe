# UX Requirements

> **Status:** In Review  
> **Last updated:** 2026-07-22  
> **Related requirements:** UX-001–UX-010, UX-021–UX-045; REQ-NF-006, REQ-NF-022  
> **Related ADRs:** ADR-0001, ADR-0003, ADR-0009–ADR-0014  
> **Open questions:** OQ-028 and platform assistive-technology matrix  
> **Dependencies:** UX research and framework spike  
> **Supersedes:** None

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| UX-001 | Every total shall navigate to its category, operation, rule trace, inputs, versions, and overrides. |
| UX-002 | Generated facts/proposals and human-entered/approved values shall have distinguishable text/icon/provenance states. |
| UX-003 | Core estimate creation/revision shall be keyboard operable with predictable focus and undo/redo. |
| UX-004 | The workspace shall support compact/comfortable density, resizable panels, saved layout, zoom/text scaling, and high-DPI. |
| UX-005 | Cost, price, margin, markup, risk, estimate, actual, draft, and approved states shall never rely on color alone. |
| UX-006 | Search, command palette, recent items, batch entry, inline editing, and templates shall support expert throughput. |
| UX-007 | Semantic names/roles/states/errors and keyboard alternatives shall expose workflows to supported assistive technology. |
| UX-008 | Import/analysis/calculation failures shall preserve valid work and present cause, scope, and recovery action. |
| UX-009 | Asynchronous recalculation shall preserve edit context and announce material changes without noisy updates. |
| UX-010 | Export, print, external link, and data-sharing actions shall preview scope/classification and avoid surprise transmission. |
| _Reserved_ | UX-011–UX-020 are intentionally reserved for future general workspace/document interaction requirements; allocate them only through this canonical file. |
| UX-021 | Viewport selection shall synchronize with textual model tree, feature, setup, operation, tool, warning, and cost evidence. |
| UX-022 | Viewport state shall show representation basis, units, analysis version, clipping/section state, and confidence warnings. |
| UX-023 | Picking/highlighting shall use stable snapshot references rather than renderer-only IDs. |
| UX-024 | Visual geometry tasks shall have a textual/tabular alternative where feasible; required visual review shall be explicit. |
| UX-025 | Feature review shall support accept/reject/edit/merge/split/manual actions while retaining original evidence. |
| UX-026 | Setup/stock/process alternatives shall be comparable without implying production feasibility. |
| UX-027 | Large-model degradation, unavailable overlays, and approximate rendering shall be visible and recoverable. |
| UX-028 | The quote workspace shall separate normal estimating, advanced analysis, and approval views while preserving shared selection/context. |
| UX-029 | Route comparison shall align alternatives by stock/setup/operation/resource, show tradeoffs/rejection reasons, and require explicit adoption. |
| UX-030 | Capacity views shall distinguish occupancy/touch/bottleneck/calendar time, data as-of/freshness, and baseline versus capacity-adjusted outputs. |
| UX-031 | Uncertainty views shall default to understandable ranges/scenarios and reveal distributions, percentiles, sources, and assumptions progressively. |
| UX-032 | Sensitivity views shall identify dominant uncertain inputs without implying causality or statistical certainty beyond the method. |
| UX-033 | Requirement coverage shall support source/location review, coverage states, impact/operation/cost linkage, conflicts, clarification, and readiness blockers. |
| UX-034 | PMI review shall distinguish semantic/graphical/imported/confirmed evidence and synchronize annotations with model geometry and requirements. |
| UX-035 | Revision comparison shall support side-by-side/synchronized/ghosted/added-removed modes plus a nonvisual change list and tolerance controls. |
| UX-036 | Revision cost explanations shall connect geometry/requirement/routing changes to time/cost/price deltas and show fixed versus refreshed rate basis. |
| UX-037 | Feature-level visualization shall offer operation/setup/tool/machine/time/cost/inspection/risk/confidence/access/revision modes with legends and approximation labels. |
| UX-038 | Availability views shall show current state, source, reservation, restriction, as-of/freshness, refresh action, and theoretical versus readiness-adjusted result. |
| UX-039 | Make/buy views shall compare internal, external, mixed, near-net, benchmark, and decline options without exposing controlled data to external services. |
| UX-040 | Correction review shall show automatic versus approved decisions, reasons/cohorts, quote-specific versus reusable scope, and proposed-rule governance. |
| UX-041 | CAM reconciliation shall align estimate/CAM/machine/actual setups, tools, times, material, inspection, cost, and variance causes with incomplete mappings visible. |
| UX-042 | Bid/no-bid and quote-priority views shall expose factors, weights, blockers, data quality, recommendation, approvals, override, and ranking cohort. |
| UX-043 | Missing/stale/low-confidence advanced data shall never look like a valid zero or neutral score and shall provide a clear refresh/confirmation path. |
| UX-044 | Every chart, heatmap, overlay, range, and comparison shall have an accessible text/table equivalent and keyboard-navigable linkage. |
| UX-045 | Approval view shall summarize price/margin, opportunity cost, delivery feasibility, requirement coverage, risk, uncertainty, unresolved assumptions, and approvals without replacing reviewer judgment. |
