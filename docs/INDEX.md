# PartProbe Documentation Index

> **Status:** In Review
> **Last updated:** 2026-09-12
> **Related requirements:** All
> **Related ADRs:** ADR-0001–ADR-0014
> **Open questions:** OQ-001–OQ-050
> **Dependencies:** Shop review and M0.2 technical spikes
> **Supersedes:** None

PartProbe is a planned local-first, cross-platform CAD-assisted machine-shop estimator. It turns models and explicit shop/customer inputs into a confidence-rated, editable routing and transparent cost/price foundation. It is not production CAM, an autonomous quote generator, or a compliance certification system.

## Coverage summaries

- **Requirements:** REQ-F-001–065, REQ-NF-001–022, UX-001–012/021–045, SEC-001–014, CALC-001–035, GEO-001–015, FEAT-001–021, TIME-001–008, DATA-001–042, and TEST-001–099 are allocated and traced in the [requirements matrix](03-requirements/requirements-matrix.md). TASK-001/002 and partial TASK-003 provide cross-platform executable evidence; no end-to-end product requirement is yet Complete.
- **Geometry:** Intake/units/hash/healing/validity/basic measurements/stock/feature/access/setup stages are specified. Fifteen STL, fifty-one 3MF, one OCCT-generated analytic STEP, one manually authored analytic STEP, and one invalid-entity STEP fixture exist. The optional local worker produces provisional exact-B-rep measurements through OCCT; bounded ASCII/binary STL plus 3MF comparison analyzers reproduce twenty-three successful mesh fixtures and exact rejections for forty-three adversarial mesh inputs. Success evidence covers all six Core units/default, direct through two-link linear component transforms, bounded model-metadata presence, five isolated legal OPC variations—including one standard internal package thumbnail and one explicit internal StartPart target-mode declaration—an index-defined 3MF open seam, and separate STL topology/intersection states with categorical confidence and conditional measurement withholding. Parser v8 uses retained Core indices for 3MF adjacency and does not weld equal-coordinate distinct indices; every mesh result exposes topology policy v1, its identity, and not-applied welding status. Failure evidence pins selected graph/reference/union/metadata/material/extension/archive/XML/resource/transform-policy, malformed geometry, duplicate/missing/external model StartPart relationships, duplicate/missing/wrong model content types, binary framing/attribute/numeric/quota, and ASCII invalid-text/grammar/empty/degenerate boundaries without geometry authority. Reflected-transform support remains deferred rather than classified as invalid. Most format fixtures remain derived from the same analytic cube or project-authored tetrahedra rather than independent CAD-tool or representative-shop evidence. Developer runtimes can be assembled and content-verified independently of Cargo output on evidenced Apple-Silicon, Ubuntu-x86_64, and Windows-x64 configurations. Supported general DAG/material semantics, broader exporter/legal-package coverage, production mesh tolerance, reviewed corpus accuracy, sandboxing, signed three-OS packaging, and product support remain absent.
- **Roadmap:** Phases 0–8 cover discovery, foundation, vertical slice, feature depth, libraries, aerospace/quality, actuals, advanced planning, and team deployment.
- **Highest risks:** Native geometry containment/fidelity, false precision in runtime/uncertainty/allocation, implausible routes, stale capacity/availability, revision ambiguity, biased learning, vendor coupling, UI complexity, sensitive-data handling, and historical replay; see [risk register](07-delivery/risk-register.md).
- **Formats:** First-class target: STEP, STL, 3MF. Experimental/secondary: IGES, OBJ. Proprietary/native formats: conversion or future licensed translator only. Current production support: none; the optional STEP worker and bounded ASCII/binary STL plus direct/linear-component-chain 3MF analyzers are provisional spike evidence only.
