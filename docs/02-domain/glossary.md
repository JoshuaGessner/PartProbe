The concise canonical vocabulary is [product terminology](../00-product/terminology.md). Additional domain terms:

- **FAI:** First article inspection; scope and governing requirement must be explicitly identified.
- **MRR:** Material-removal rate; a coarse process input, not cycle time by itself.
- **NRE:** Non-recurring engineering or one-time cost.
- **OBB/AABB:** Oriented/axis-aligned bounding box.
- **Prove-out:** Controlled effort validating a new program/setup before normal production.
- **Rate card:** Approved, effective-dated financial rates distinct from machine capability.
- **Source model:** Original imported bytes retained with hash and revision context.
- **Stock-to-part ratio:** Starting stock mass or volume divided by finished part mass or volume; label basis.
- **Workcenter:** Configurable resource/cost grouping; may represent one machine or a qualified class.
