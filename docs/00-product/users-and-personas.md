## Primary persona: estimator

Owns RFQ intake, assumptions, quantity breaks, routing edits, cost review, and draft pricing. Needs speed, keyboard efficiency, visible uncertainty, and reproducible calculations.

## Decision and review personas

- **Senior estimator:** approves unusual methods, risk, and price overrides.
- **CNC programmer / manufacturing engineer:** validates setup, tool, machine, and runtime assumptions.
- **Machinist:** contributes practical workholding, handling, and prove-out knowledge.
- **Quality inspector / manager:** prices inspection, FAI, documentation, and traceability effort.
- **Purchasing:** maintains material and outside-process evidence and expiration dates.
- **Sales / shop manager:** manages bid strategy, approval, customer presentation, and margins.
- **Administrator:** manages users, configuration, backup, and deployment boundaries.
- **Read-only reviewer:** inspects approved evidence without changing it.

## Research rule

These are provisional roles, not authorization policy. Validate actual role overlap, separation of duties, approval thresholds, and controlled-data access during interviews before accepting the permissions model.
