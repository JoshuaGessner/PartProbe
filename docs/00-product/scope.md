## Initial product scope

Local-first RFQ and quote management; revisioned input packages; STEP/STL/3MF intake; unit confirmation; model validation and basic geometry; material and stock selection; one coarse process/setup/runtime proposal; editable approved routing; basic requirement checklist; manual availability and make/buy fields; cost, risk, quantity-break, and pricing calculations; override/audit history; save/reopen; internal estimate and customer quote previews.

## Later planned scope

Feature-based estimation, multiple routing alternatives, capacity/delivery feasibility, opportunity-cost decision support, probabilistic/scenario analysis, model/feature/cost revision comparison, full manufacturing-requirement coverage, experimental structured PMI/MBD, feature cost/risk visualization, correction learning, CAM reconciliation, availability-aware estimates, sourcing analysis, bid/priority scoring, shop libraries, quality depth, controlled calibration, advanced geometry/process planning, and team/LAN deployment. Placement and maturity are authoritative in the roadmap.

## Cross-cutting scope

Windows, Linux, and macOS; explicit units; fixed-precision money; deterministic versioned rules; source hashes; algorithm snapshots; confidence reasons; migration-safe persistence; no required public cloud.

Scope is phased in [the roadmap](../07-delivery/roadmap.md); inclusion here does not mean implemented.
