# Initial Non-Goals

> **Status:** In Review  
> **Last updated:** 2026-07-22  
> **Related requirements:** REQ-NF-008, REQ-NF-009, REQ-NF-019–REQ-NF-022; SEC-001, SEC-013  
> **Related ADRs:** ADR-0004, ADR-0005, ADR-0009–ADR-0014  
> **Open questions:** OQ-021, OQ-030–OQ-050  
> **Dependencies:** None  
> **Supersedes:** None

Deferred from initial releases:

- Production-safe G-code generation or full CAM replacement.
- Perfect feature recognition or guaranteed setup planning.
- Automatic authoritative tolerance/GD&T extraction.
- Native import for every proprietary CAD format.
- ERP replacement, full scheduling, or machine monitoring.
- Claims of automatic regulatory compliance.
- Unreviewed AI quotes or external AI upload of technical data.
- Public instant quoting, mobile apps, or cloud-only deployment.
- Automatic changes to approved shop defaults from historical data.
- Production scheduling, dispatching, MRP, or ERP replacement from the capacity engine.
- Treating opportunity cost as accounting cost or automatically raising price because capacity is constrained.
- Presenting scenario/three-point/simulation outputs as validated probabilities without evidence.
- Automatically adopting an optimized routing, make/buy option, bid/no-bid recommendation, or learned rule.
- Recalculating historical approved quotes when algorithms, availability, capacity, or rates change.
- Hard dependency on one CAM vendor, scheduler, marketplace, ERP, or QMS.
- Automatic upload of models or derived technical data for supplier/marketplace benchmarking.

Reconsidering an item requires a scoped proposal, threat and validation analysis, and an ADR.
